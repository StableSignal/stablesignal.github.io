MigrationKit validation boundary

The private, local-first wizard completed an internal representative workflow check, its 14-test suite, demo generation, artifact hashing, and cross-link verification. A real operator still needs to run one sanitized workflow and confirm that the questions, outputs, and verifier are understandable without engineering assistance.

A possible MigrationReceiptRegistry has been documented as design-only. The current direction is a minimal, permissionless hash anchor that must never contain personally identifiable information, workflow names, URLs, scores, or private artifacts. No registry contract has been implemented or deployed, and an anchor would not prove approval, correctness, or migration completion.

Why AgentPay remains queued

AgentPay v0 begins only after StableScore reaches its seven-day evidence gate and MigrationKit passes a human operator-validation session. Keeping that dependency explicit prevents a new product surface from outrunning the evidence and operator feedback it depends on.
